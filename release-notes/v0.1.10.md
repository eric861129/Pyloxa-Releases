# Pyloxa 0.1.10

This update opens the Pro preview: sign in and use every Pro feature for free until Pro goes on sale. The free version still needs no account. It also reorganizes navigation and settings, and adds optional anonymous usage statistics.

## Pro preview

- **Sign in to use Pro for free.** Choose **Sign in** in **Settings → License** or on any Pro lock, and sign in in your browser with Google, GitHub or a code sent to your email. The sidebar shows **Pro preview** while it lasts.
- **The preview ends when Pro goes on sale.** We will tell you at least 30 days before, in the app and by email. After it ends you keep Pro for 14 more days and see the early-bird discount in the app and on your account page. Then the app returns to the free version and keeps your data and settings.
- **What Pro includes:** several projects working at once, with one task center for everything waiting on you; decisions within the limits you set (automatic mode); automatic fallbacks when quota runs out; automatic cross-testing; brain fallbacks; Agent Teams (experimental); project priority, per-connection quotas and scheduled plans; a usage dashboard and reports; notifications to Telegram, Discord or a webhook; a skills library synced to every CLI and assigned per project or profile; and routing packs.
- **Two computers per account.** Signing in on a third computer asks you to remove one. Your account page at `account.pyloxa.com/account` lists your computers and lets you rename or remove them, download your data or delete the account.
- **Works offline for a while.** Pyloxa refreshes the license about once a day. If it can't reach the license service, Pro keeps working for 14 days, and from day 3 the sidebar says how many days are left.

## Changes

- **Two-level navigation.** The sidebar now holds your work: new conversation, task center, run history and conversations. Agents, rules, commands, skills, memory, connections and appearance are grouped under Settings.
- **Agents and brain settings in four sections:** brain, routing, verification and cross-testing, and advanced, each with a summary of the current settings. Long explanations moved into help bubbles.
- **Plan cards put results first.** When you expand a task, its results and the accept button come before the routing instructions, and you can switch between compact and detailed views.
- **More consistent pages.** Settings pages share one header and width, tabs look the same everywhere, tooltips replace browser tooltips, help text is larger, and pages show loading placeholders and empty states.
- **Send with Enter or Ctrl+Enter**, your choice. The new-conversation box is centered.
- The view panel no longer repeats buttons and remembers its tab and width.

## New

- **Arrange the sidebar your way.** Drag to resize it or collapse it to icons, pin settings pages you use often, and reorder or hide the items under Work.
- **Find conversations faster.** The conversation list is grouped by date, and you can search, pin, archive and right-click conversations.
- **A color for each project.**
- **Prompt templates.** Type `/` in the message box to pick a saved prompt; mark the parts you change each time with `{{…}}`. The free version keeps 5 templates; Pro has no limit.
- **Custom keyboard shortcuts** in **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts**. The app menu follows your choices, and Pyloxa asks before replacing a shortcut that is already in use.
- **Arrange the task center:** reorder its sections and choose which ones to show.
- All layout and display options are free.

## Privacy

- **New requests, only after you sign in.** Signing in opens your browser at `account.pyloxa.com`. After that, Pyloxa refreshes its license about once a day, sending only a device ID, a device credential and the Pyloxa and Windows versions. The free version never connects to the license service.
- **Optional anonymous usage statistics.** After the first task you accept, Pyloxa asks once whether to share anonymous usage statistics. It is off unless you turn it on, in **Settings → Usage and feedback**. When on, Pyloxa sends one report a day to `stats.pyloxa.com` with counts and categories only, never your prompts, code, file names, paths or account. You can preview each report, reset the anonymous ID or delete your data there.
- **Notifications (Pro) go straight from your computer** to the Telegram bot, Discord webhook or HTTPS webhook you set up, with the project name, the event and the plan or task title.
- The [privacy policy](https://pyloxa.com/privacy/) has a new section on Pro accounts.

## Upgrading

Run the new installer over the old version. Your run history, projects, settings, shared memory and saved API keys in `%APPDATA%\Pyloxa` are kept. The database stays at version 21, so you can go back to 0.1.9 with the same data.

## Known limitations

- Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit only.
- The installer is not code-signed. SmartScreen may warn you; choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- No automatic updates for Pyloxa itself. Pyloxa tells you when a new version is out.
- Codex runs through `codex exec` in its sandbox. On Windows, the sandbox usually can't build projects or install packages, so Codex's results are checked by your verification commands.
- Test code that an agent writes still runs outside the sandbox when your verification commands run it, without your keys in its environment. Review the changes of tasks that add or change tests.
- Gemini CLI support is experimental. It can't act as the brain or as a cross-tester, and it can't run commands in unattended mode.
- Agent Teams is experimental.
- Tested with Claude Code 2.1.318, Codex CLI 0.166.0 and Gemini CLI 0.66.0 and 0.67.0.

# Pyloxa 0.1.10（繁體中文）

這次更新開放 Pro 預覽：登入就能免費使用所有 Pro 功能，直到 Pro 開賣。免費版照舊不需要帳號。這一版也重新整理了導覽與設定，並加入選擇加入的匿名使用統計。

## Pro 預覽

- **登入就能免費使用 Pro。** 在「設定 → 授權」或任何 Pro 鎖頭按「登入」，在瀏覽器以 Google、GitHub 或寄到 email 的驗證碼登入。預覽期間側欄會顯示「Pro 預覽」。
- **預覽在 Pro 開賣時結束。** 我們會至少提前 30 天在 App 與 email 通知。結束後你還有 14 天的 Pro，並在 App 與帳號頁看到早鳥折扣；之後 App 回到免費版，資料與設定都保留。
- **Pro 包含：** 多個專案同時進行，等你處理的事集中在任務中心；在你設定的範圍內自動決定（自動模式）；額度用完時自動備援；自動交叉測試；大腦備援；Agent Teams（實驗）；專案優先序、每條連線的配額與排定時間的計畫；用量儀表板與報告；通知到 Telegram、Discord 或 webhook；同步到各 CLI、依專案或 Profile 指派的 Skills 庫；派工包。
- **每個帳號 2 台電腦。** 在第三台登入時會請你移除一台。帳號頁 `account.pyloxa.com/account` 列出你的電腦，可以改名、移除、下載你的資料或刪除帳號。
- **離線也能用一陣子。** Pyloxa 大約每天更新一次授權。連不到授權服務時，Pro 還能用 14 天，第 3 天起側欄會顯示剩下幾天。

## 調整

- **導覽分成兩層。** 側欄只放工作：新對話、任務中心、執行紀錄與對話。Agent、規則、命令、Skills、記憶、連線與外觀集中到「設定」。
- **「Agent 與大腦」分成四區**：大腦、派工、驗收與交叉測試、進階，並顯示目前設定的摘要；長說明收進說明氣泡。
- **計畫卡先看成果。** 展開工作時，成果與接受按鈕在派工指示前面，可以切換精簡與詳細。
- **頁面更一致。** 設定類的頁面共用頁首與寬度，分頁的樣子統一，提示改用樣式化的氣泡，說明文字變大，載入中與沒有資料時有對應的畫面。
- **可以選 Enter 或 Ctrl+Enter 送出**；新對話的輸入框置中。
- 檢視面板不再重複按鈕，並記住分頁與寬度。

## 新功能

- **側欄照你的習慣排。** 可以拖曳調寬或收成圖示列，把常用的設定頁釘到側欄，「工作」區的項目可以調整順序與隱藏。
- **更快找到對話。** 對話清單依時間分組，可以搜尋、釘選、封存，也可以按右鍵操作。
- **每個專案可以有自己的顏色。**
- **常用指令範本。** 在輸入框打「/」選用存好的指令，用 `{{…}}` 標出每次要換的地方。免費版可以存 5 個範本，Pro 不限。
- **自訂快捷鍵**：在「設定 → 快捷鍵」。應用程式選單跟著換；和已經在用的快捷鍵衝突時會先問你。
- **任務中心的版面**：區塊可以調整順序與顯示。
- 版面與顯示的客製化全部免費。

## 隱私

- **新的網路請求，只在你登入之後。** 登入時用瀏覽器開啟 `account.pyloxa.com`；之後 Pyloxa 大約每天更新一次授權，只送裝置 ID、裝置憑證與 Pyloxa、Windows 的版本。免費版不會連到授權服務。
- **選擇加入的匿名使用統計。** 你第一次接受工作成果後，Pyloxa 會問一次要不要分享匿名使用統計。預設關閉，在「設定 → 使用統計與回報」開關。開啟後每天送一份報告到 `stats.pyloxa.com`，只有次數與類別，不含提示內容、程式碼、檔名、路徑或帳號；在同一頁可以預覽每份報告、重設匿名 ID 或刪除你的資料。
- **通知（Pro）從你的電腦直接送出**，到你設定的 Telegram bot、Discord webhook 或 HTTPS webhook，內容是專案名稱、事件與計畫或工作的標題。
- [隱私權政策](https://pyloxa.com/zh-TW/privacy/)新增了 Pro 帳號一節。

## 升級

直接執行新的安裝檔覆蓋舊版即可。存在 `%APPDATA%\Pyloxa` 的執行紀錄、專案、設定、共用記憶與保存的 API Key 都會保留。資料庫維持第 21 版，所以可以用同一份資料回到 0.1.9。

## 已知限制

- 只支援 Windows 10 與 11（64 位元）。
- 安裝檔沒有程式碼簽章，SmartScreen 可能跳出警告；請按「其他資訊」，再按「仍要執行」。
- Pyloxa 本身不會自動更新。有新版本時 Pyloxa 會提示你。
- Codex 透過 `codex exec` 在它的沙箱內執行。在 Windows 上，這個沙箱通常無法建置專案或安裝套件，所以 Codex 的成果以你的驗證命令驗收。
- Agent 寫的測試程式，在驗證命令執行它時仍在沙箱外執行（環境裡沒有你的金鑰）。新增或修改測試的工作，請看過變更再接受。
- Gemini CLI 是實驗性支援：不能擔任大腦或交叉測試的測試者，在無人值守模式下也不能執行命令。
- Agent Teams 是實驗功能。
- 已測試 Claude Code 2.1.318、Codex CLI 0.166.0，以及 Gemini CLI 0.66.0 與 0.67.0。
